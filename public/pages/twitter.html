4. Technical Deep Dive: HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) & Bitrate Selection

Twitter encodes video using HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) wrapped in .m3u8 playlist manifests. When you watch a video on X, your client requests a master playlist containing multiple bandwidth tiers:

  • 320 × 568 (low mobile bandwidth ~256 kbps)
  • 720 × 1280 (standard HD ~832 kbps)
  • 1080 × 1920 (full high bitrate ~2.1 Mbps)

Many low-quality downloaders naively grab the first stream listed in the manifest (which is often the lowest 240p or 360p resolution!). SaverFrom's backend parses the entire m3u8 playlist, compares the declared bandwidth attributes, and downloads the highest available bitrate chunk stream.
