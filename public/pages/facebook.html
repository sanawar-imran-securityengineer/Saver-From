7. Troubleshooting Facebook Download Issues

Encountered Error Root Cause Resolution Steps
Video Not Found / Private Error Post privacy set to 'Friends' or hosted in a private group. Check the video post in an incognito window. If you cannot see it without logging in, the video is private and cannot be downloaded.
Download Returns Lower Quality The creator uploaded the original media in 720p or standard definition. SaverFrom always extracts the highest quality available on Facebook servers. If HD was not encoded by Meta, only SD can be retrieved.
fb.watch Link Fails to Expand Malformed share query or expired short link. Open the video in your Facebook app, tap 'Copy Link' again, or open in a browser to get the full facebook.com/watch/?v=... URL.
Audio Out of Sync Variable framerate encoding in older Facebook live stream recordings. Play the video in VLC Media Player, which automatically adjusts PTS timecode offsets.
